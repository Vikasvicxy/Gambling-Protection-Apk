Shield blocks gambling where it actually happens: at the DNS layer, on your device, before a betting site can load a single pixel.

▌BLOCKING THAT HOLDS
• On-device DNS blocking through Android's local VPN. No browser extension, no safe-browsing list you have to trust.
• Leak-proof by design. Queries are answered or refused on the phone. Your browsing history is never uploaded anywhere, because it never leaves.
• Gambling apps are blocked too, not just websites.
• Per-app split tunneling: exempt a banking or work app that will not run inside another VPN, and everything else stays filtered. The trade-off is stated where you choose.
• IPv6 leak protection: answers IPv6 lookups with an empty reply so your phone falls back to IPv4, where Shield is actually in the path. Without it, a device on native IPv6 could reach blocked sites without passing the filter.
• App risk scan: sees through the apps Android lets Shield look at, for known betting and casino wording, entirely offline. It shows what it matched and takes no action: nothing uploaded, nothing deleted.
• Signed, automatically updating blocklists, with a local seed list so protection works the moment you install, even offline.
• Your own exceptions list. Blocking a site you actually need is one tap to fix.
• One-tap Safe Search assistance for Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo. Offered, not enforced: Shield cannot force Safe Search under encryption, so set it in the engine's own settings, which is the only place it genuinely filters.

▌WHEN THE URGE HITS
• A 15-minute cooling-off timer that stands between you and a decision you would rather make later.
• A typed pledge, not just a button. The friction is the point.
• Fortress windows: schedule lock-downs for the hours you know are dangerous.
• Guardian PIN. Turning protection off takes a deliberate extra step, not a stray tap.

▌RECOVERY, TRACKED HONESTLY
• Day count and a money-saved estimate from your own weekly spend — calculated from the days Shield was actually running, not from days on the calendar, with the coverage percentage shown next to it. A day with no protection earns nothing.
• Craving journal with triggers, so you can see the pattern instead of guessing at it.
• One-tap sobriety PDF report, generated locally, for a counsellor or a sponsor.
• Milestones that mark how far you have actually come.

▌YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• No ads. No analytics. No crash reporters. No tracking. Every dependency is Jetpack, AndroidX, or built by us.
• Encrypted backup and restore. Exports are sealed with AES-256-GCM and a key only you hold. Forget the passphrase and the file is unreadable, by anyone, including us.
• Your Guardian PIN is never written into a backup.
• Accountability pairing is entirely optional and off by default.

▌BUILT FOR THE HARD DAYS
• An offline crisis and helpline directory, so the number you need is never behind a network request.
• Honest, plain-language copy. No shame, no streaks to lose over a missed day.

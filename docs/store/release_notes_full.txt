Shield v1.1.0 - a feature release, and a more honest one.

NEW
- Per-app split tunneling. Exempt a banking or work app that will not run
  inside another VPN, and Shield keeps filtering everything else. The trade-off
  is stated plainly where you make the choice.
- IPv6 leak protection. Answers IPv6 (AAAA) lookups with an empty reply so your
  phone falls back to IPv4, where Shield is actually in the path. Previously a
  device on native IPv6 could reach blocked sites without passing the filter.
- App risk scan. Looks through the apps Shield can see for known betting and
  casino wording, entirely on this phone, and shows what it matched. Nothing is
  uploaded and nothing is deleted: it is a list of things worth a look.
- One-tap Safe Search assistance for Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo.

HONESTY CHANGES
- Money saved is now calculated from the days Shield was actually running
  rather than from days on the calendar, and the coverage percentage is shown
  next to it. A day with no protection earns nothing. The streak is unchanged.
- Safe Search is offered, not enforced. Shield cannot force Safe Search at DNS
  level, because over an encrypted connection the search engine still sees the
  address you typed. Set Safe Search in the engine's own settings; that is the
  only place it genuinely filters.
- The app risk scan covers only the apps Android lets Shield see. It is not a
  full inventory of your phone.

FIXES
- The IPv6 gap is closed by suppressing AAAA rather than by routing IPv6 into a
  tunnel that forwards no packets, which would have broken IPv6 entirely.

No ads, no analytics, no crash reporters, no tracking. The risk scan is fully
offline, and the savings figure never leaves the device.

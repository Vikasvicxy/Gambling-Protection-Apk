Shield v1.2.0

STEADIER ON BAD NETWORKS
- Blocklist updates now retry across three CDNs, and keep the last known-good list if every origin fails. Blocking never stops while offline.
- DNS is briefly held during a network change instead of failing while the new route settles.

BATTERY
- One tap opens your vendor's autostart screen (MIUI, Oppo, Vivo, Huawei, Samsung and more) so the VPN is not killed in the background.

No account, no telemetry, no installed-app uploads.
